## Overview

Quiet, personal and deliberately understated. User explicitly requested less prominent text. The supplied running artwork leads; copy stays conversational and brief.

## Colors

Warm paper, muted dark text, readable secondary text and pale dividers. Rust is reserved for interaction. Avoid bold black headings.

## Typography

Self-hosted Manrope with native Chinese sans. Regular weight throughout. Greeting is 21px (20px mobile); wordmark 18px; product names 16px (15px mobile); descriptions 13px (12px mobile); metadata 11px. No oversized declarations or promotional slogans.

## Layout

Centered 792px outer container with 36px gutters, changing to 24px at 600px. Desktop intro pairs small text with a 228px portrait and 48px gap. Mobile stacks text and a 210px-wide portrait, keeping the artwork compact. Open product rows have 34px marks, flexible text and 16px arrows.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat, without shadows. Fine horizontal dividers separate the products.

## Shapes

Intact 3:4 artwork with 3px corner radius. Product marks use 10px rounded squares.

## Components

Language buttons remain text-only. Whole product rows link to the supplied websites. Hover and focus move arrows 2px right and up over 200ms. Keyboard focus uses a 2px rust outline. Reduced-motion disables transitions. Footer contains copyright only.

## Do's and Don'ts

Keep copy short, light and readable. Preserve bilingual parity and product order. Do not restore the large identity statement, image caption, promotional section note or footer slogan.
